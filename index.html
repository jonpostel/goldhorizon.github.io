About Gold Horizon

Gold Horizon is focused on building the orbital infrastructure that future missions depend on.

We design and operate modular platforms that support in‑orbit assembly, refueling, inspection and repair, enabling long‑duration operations across Earth orbit and cislunar space.

Our approach emphasizes safety, interoperability and sustainability, working with partners to make space operations more resilient and responsible.
